The first Gate 9 pass found one **MAJOR prose-standard issue**:

The story logic, mystery timing, and continuity were correct, but too many ordinary sentences were separated into one-line paragraphs.

That conflicted with the locked Writing Rules:
- normal multi-sentence paragraphs are the default;
- one-line paragraphs are reserved for emphasis, punchlines, surprise, emotional beats, or supernatural changes.

The manuscript was revised to:
- combine ordinary narration into natural paragraphs;
- preserve short lines only where tension/comedy/reveal earns them;
- keep the exact four-scene structure;
- preserve all dialogue content;
- preserve the same mystery information and endpoint.

Gate 9 was then rerun.
