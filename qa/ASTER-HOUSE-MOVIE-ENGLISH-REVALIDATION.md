- natural spoken English;
- simple, playable lines;
- reaction before explanation;
- interruption, hesitation, silence, redirection, and imperfect answers where natural;

- character-specific humor rather than generic jokes;
- technical exposition carried through conflict, correction, consequence, or visual action;
