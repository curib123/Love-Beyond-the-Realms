# Revalidation History

Originality Revision v2 changed a foundation-level opening mechanic.

The pre-v2 opening used:
- Lily directly seeing Max;
- Max hearing Lily while not seeing her;
- a ghost-like handsome stranger as the opening visual hook.

That material is superseded.

The v2 opening now uses:

**ROOM → WRITING → VOICE → FACE**

During revalidation, one wording ambiguity was corrected in the World Bible:

Old Phase Lag wording could be read as allowing Lily to directly see Max's earlier action before P1.

It now specifies a **room-state trace** and explicitly states that Phase Lag cannot bypass the perception ladder.

Gate 1 criteria were also expanded to permanently test the v2 originality lock.
