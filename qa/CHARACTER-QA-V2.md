# Minor Notes

## NOTE-001 — Do Not Make Note-Writing Too Similar to Spoken Banter

Written exchanges should initially feel different from later voice conversations.

Early notes may be:
- shorter;
- more cautious;
- more deliberate;
- easier to misread.

Voice contact should reveal new rhythm, hesitation, timing, and emotional leakage.

**Severity:** MINOR / NON-BLOCKING  
**Owner:** A093 Dialogue Editor + A048 Romantic Comedy Editor

## NOTE-002 — First-Sight Attraction Must Stay Proportional

When P1 first sight occurs, it may create awkward physical awareness.

Do not let one visual reveal instantly jump the relationship from curiosity/trust into strong romantic certainty.

The emotional bond must continue developing after first sight.

**Severity:** MINOR / NON-BLOCKING  
**Owner:** A043 Lily Guardian + A044 Max Guardian + A048 Romantic Comedy Editor
