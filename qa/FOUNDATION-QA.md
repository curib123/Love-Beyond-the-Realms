# Gate 1 Checklist

## 1. Series Identity

**Requirement:** Romantic Comedy + Mystery + Sci-Fi identity is explicit.

**PASS**

The Series Constitution defines the priority as:

**Romantic Comedy → Character Drama → Mystery → Science Fiction / Supernatural Stakes**

Romantic comedy is a structural genre requirement rather than optional decoration.

## 2. Equal Main Characters

**Requirement:** Lily and Max are equal protagonists.

**PASS**

Lily Marlowe and Max Arden are explicitly defined as equal main characters with:
- independent goals;
- independent relationships;
- flaws;
- mysteries;
- victories;
- mistakes;
- regular POV ownership.

Lily remains the opening audience-entry character without reducing Max to an interlude-only role.

## 3. Simple Close-Third POV

**Requirement:** simple close-third POV is formally defined.

**PASS**

The Series Constitution locks:
- Lily POV;
- Max POV;
- close third-person;
- one POV per scene;
- no uncontrolled head-hopping.

The Character Bible supports distinct Lily and Max narrative identities.

## 4. Ordinary Life Exists in Both Worlds

**Requirement:** both realities support life outside the dimensional mystery.

**PASS**

Earth/Bellweather supports:
- work;
- family;
- friendship;
- education decisions;
- ordinary urban life.

Velaire/Rivenhall supports:
- family;
- employment;
- archives;
- transport;
- civic systems;
- independent culture and technology.

Neither world exists only to serve the romance.

## 5. Split-History Foundation

**Requirement:** Earth and Velaire have a coherent relationship.

**PASS**

Writer truth establishes that:
- they were once one reality;
- the Twinfall created two stable histories;
- both branches are real;
- neither branch is morally or ontologically "fake."

This gives the series a distinct long-term science-fiction mystery.

## 6. Echo Exchange

**Requirement:** Echo Exchange is defined.

**PASS**

Echo Exchange can affect:
- object position;
- surface state;
- color;
- temperature;
- minor damage;
- sound;
- light;
- temporary room geometry.

Clear limits prevent unlimited duplication, resurrection, effortless matter creation, or consequence-free problem solving.

## 7. Phase Lag

**Requirement:** Phase Lag is defined.

**PASS**

Normal, unstable, and rare pre-echo behavior are distinguished.

Pre-echo is explicitly restricted because of causality and mystery-fairness risk.

## 8. Shared Minute

**Requirement:** Shared Minute is defined.

**PASS**

Shared Minute is a rare high-stability alignment that may temporarily improve:
- mutual sight;
- shared object state;
- physical influence;
- later contact possibilities.

It is not automatically sixty seconds and is not a romance-powered event.

## 9. Costs and Limits

**Requirement:** dimensional mechanics have meaningful restrictions.

**PASS**

Established costs include:
- cold;
- headaches;
- nausea;
- shaking;
- sleep loss;
- memory fuzz;
- sensory doubling;
- lost minutes;
- object damage;
- room instability.

Repeated use can increase local instability.

Crossing progression and perception progression are tiered rather than immediately available.

## 10. Lily Is Not Chosen

**Requirement:** no chosen-one shortcut.

**PASS**

Lily's access results from:
- location;
- overlap geometry;
- phase orientation;
- mirrorstone coherence.

She has no supernatural destiny or exclusive magical bloodline.

## 11. Max Is Alive

**Requirement:** Max is not secretly a conventional ghost.

**PASS**

Max is a living Velaire human who became phase-bound during the Larkspur incident.

This preserves the intended "ghost-like at first, sci-fi truth later" structure.

## 12. Lily's Parents Remain Outside the Conspiracy

**Requirement:** grief is not retroactively converted into mystery bait.

**PASS**

Daniel and Elise Marlowe died in an ordinary road accident.

Their deaths are explicitly protected from:
- Max;
- Velaire;
- Aster House;
- the dimensional conspiracy.

## 13. No Locked Final Chapter Count

**Requirement:** series remains open-ended.

**PASS**

The Series Constitution and Roadmap explicitly state:
- final chapter count is UNLOCKED;
- final volume count is UNLOCKED;
- only the active arc receives detailed planning.

## 14. Future Arcs Remain Open

**Requirement:** no premature final route.

**PASS**

Only the **Aster House Arc** is selected.

Future arcs remain **UNSELECTED / UNLOCKED** and must arise from existing canon.
