# 2. Reveal-Protection Rules

**RESERVED** does not mean "free to use whenever useful."

A reserved or high-risk location requires both:
1. story need;
2. knowledge/reveal authorization.

## LOC-006 — East Maintenance Corridor

Allowed before major reveals:
- old construction;
- sealed access;
- ordinary repair history;
- strange but unexplained material/temperature/electrical behavior.

Protected:
- definitive mirrorstone explanation;
- proof of common pre-Twinfall structure;
- direct MT-001 confirmation.

## LOC-009 — Rivenhall Civic Archive

Allowed:
- Max's ordinary work;
- public maps;
- mundane building records;
- incomplete anomaly references when authorized.

Protected:
- restricted Threshold Keeper records;
- responsible researcher identity for MT-002;
- definitive Twinfall truth;
- any record that would bypass required clue paths.

## LOC-010 — Arden Family Home

Allowed when arc logic authorizes:
- Mina and Elara's independent lives;
- family consequence;
- evidence that does not automatically reach Max.

Protected:
- Mina's survival from Max until an authorized information path exists;
- exposition that bypasses Max's uncertainty;
- automatic contact with Earth.

## LOC-012 — The Hollow Between

Protected until system and arc state authorize:
- physical entry;
- reliable navigation;
- major historical visions;
- easy information retrieval;
- shortcut crossing.

The Hollow must never become an exposition room that answers mysteries for free.

# 3. Ordinary-Life Location Protection

Ordinary-life locations are required to keep both protagonists from existing only inside the dimensional plot.

Earth ordinary-life anchors:
- LOC-003 Paper Moon Books & Café;
- LOC-004 Bellweather Riverfront;
- LOC-005 Nora's Home.

Velaire ordinary-life anchors:
- ordinary portions of LOC-007 Larkspur House;
- LOC-010 Arden Family Home when authorized;
- LOC-011 Rivenhall Market Bridges;
- public/work portions of LOC-009 Rivenhall Civic Archive.

Using a location for ordinary life does not automatically activate its mystery content.

# 4. Aster / Larkspur Dual-POV Function

The opening location pair is intentionally asymmetric.

## Lily side

Aster House / Unit 4B gives Lily:
- a physical home;
- independence pressure;
- landlord/neighbour consequences;
- Earth-side clues;
- the ability to observe Max through P0 Bleed.

## Max side

Larkspur House / Room 17 gives Max:
- a physical Velaire-side reference point;
- incident consequences;
- his own investigation space;
- Velaire-side clues;
- the ability to hear/sense Lily under P0.

## Shared function

The pair supports both regular POVs without requiring both protagonists to occupy the same physical world.

A POV scene must stay physically and informationally consistent with its owner.

# 5. Opening Active Arc

**Selected cluster:** Aster House + Unit 4B + controlled Larkspur/Room 17 POV scenes.

**Arc name:** Aster House Arc

Purpose:
- establish Lily;
- establish Max;
- establish both POV voices;
- establish the roommate romantic-comedy engine;
- prove repeatable dimensional behavior;
- seed Echo Exchange;
- support fair clues toward deeper mystery;
- keep high-level Twinfall truth protected.

"Controlled Larkspur/Room 17 POV scenes" means Max may have regular POV scenes, but those scenes may not access protected Velaire information simply because they occur on his side.

This selection does not lock the next arc.

# 6. Future Selection Rule

A future location may become an active arc only when:
1. current canon naturally points toward it;
2. mystery dependencies allow it;
3. it supports character and romantic development;
4. it introduces something new rather than repeating Aster House;
5. selection does not require fixing the final series length.

No current RESERVED or UNUSED location is automatically the next arc.

Future route remains **UNLOCKED**.
