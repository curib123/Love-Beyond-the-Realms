# 1. Arc Identity

The Aster House Arc proved that Lily and Max are real, established bounded communication, earned narrow P1, and ended with a safer P0 baseline.

The Shared Foundations Arc asks the next natural question:

**Does the Aster/Larkspur correlation extend beyond Unit 4B and Room 17 into the older structure of the buildings themselves?**

The arc is not about discovering a new person, earning first sight again, or escalating toward touch.

Its identity is:

**two established cross-world roommates become cautious field partners and discover that the buildings correspond more deeply than they thought.**

The local answer may become:

**yes — deeper structural correspondence exists.**

The arc may NOT answer:

**why the two worlds share that structure.**

- opening wrong doors;
- touching through the Veil;
- live simultaneous manipulation.
