# 1. Roadmap Rules

This roadmap obeys the Gate 6 architecture.

Hard ceilings:
- relationship endpoint no higher than **R2 — Cross-World Roommates**;
- default perception remains **P0**, with narrow-condition **P1** available;
- **C0** remains unchanged;
- **X0** remains unchanged;
- no P2/P3;
- no C1+;
- no X1+;
- no MT-001/002/003 reveal;
- Mina's survival is not confirmed to Max;
- Max remains phase-bound;
- no unrestricted Max movement through Rivenhall;
- no Hollow entry;
- no manuscript F-ID is marked PLANTED during planning.

Authorized foreshadowing candidates:
- F-001 Mirror Delay;
- F-002 Wrong Furniture;
- F-003 Cold Brass Key.

All remain **PLANNED — NOT YET PLANTED** until actual prose exists.

# 2. POV Distribution

Planned chapter POV count:
- **Lily:** 11 chapters
- **Max:** 9 chapters

This distribution is intentional, not a quota.

Lily carries:
- opening audience entry;
- Earth ordinary-life pressure;
- apartment consequences;
- final independent choice.

Max carries:
- Velaire-side reality;
- phase-bound limitation;
- timing/perception experiments;
- midpoint first sight;
- part of the climax;
- family-separation aftermath.

No chapter replays the full events of a previous chapter from the other POV.
