# 4. Chapter Roadmap

## CH-001 — A Place of My Own
**POV:** Lily

Lily moves into Unit 4B and makes it emotionally hers.

At night, the first undeniable anomaly is **environmental, not a person**:
the window/light briefly belongs to an unfamiliar city/room state.

Ending:
Lily realizes the apartment did not contain a stranger.

For a moment, it contained **someone else's reality**.

No F-ID assigned.

---

## CH-002 — Someone Else Is Using My Room
**POV:** Lily

Lily tries to reproduce/rationalize the room anomaly without formal science language.

A second state event leaves writing/information that cannot be hers.

She leaves a short challenge/question on a corresponding surface.

Later, a different handwriting answers.

Ending:
**I WAS ABOUT TO ASK YOU THAT.**

M-001 progression:
already ACTIVE from CH-001's impossible environmental anomaly; CH-002 deepens it by adding evidence of an intentional responder.

No faces.
No voice.
No F-ID.

## CH-003 — The Note That Shouldn't Exist
**POV:** Max

First Max POV.

He receives Lily's impossible writing through Room 17.

His humor hides fear that Room 17 or his mind is worsening.

Ending:
Lily answers something Max did not provide.

M-001 deepens.

## CH-004 — The Other Tenant
**POV:** Lily
