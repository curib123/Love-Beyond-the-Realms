# 4. Chapter Roadmap

## CH-021 — The Inspection Notice

**POV:** Lily  
**Movement:** A — Permission to Enter

Purpose:
- establish the post-Aster R2 routine without replaying CH-020;
- show Lily returning to work/home responsibilities;
- bring Inez's practical maintenance process forward;
- create the legitimate path into LOC-006.

Core beats:
- Lily receives notice that Inez will inspect the east-side service area because of the prior door/frame, temperature, and service complaints;
- Lily realizes the inspection may finally expose the wall area adjacent to Unit 4B;
- at the communication base, Lily and Max agree this is an investigation opportunity only if it does not override work, sleep, or Inez's rules;
- their Roommate Agreement becomes the starting research discipline rather than a joke prop.

Relationship:
- R2 stable;
- banter shows comfort, not romantic escalation.

Mystery:
- no new M-ID conclusion.

System:
- P0 only required.

Ending:
Inez gives Lily a specific inspection time and tells her to bring the notes/photos from the earlier complaint.

## CH-022 — The Safe Distance

**POV:** Max  
**Movement:** A — Permission to Enter

Purpose:
- define Max's field range in prose before the investigation depends on it;
- show his frustration with phase-bound mobility;
- prove he can stop instead of forcing progress.

Core beats:
- Max surveys Room 17, the adjoining service passage, and the first structural junction/inspection alcove;
- he records the first onset of boundary symptoms at the outer edge;
- he stops when the stop condition is met;
- he prepares a fixed measurement protocol for Lily and himself.

Character:
- Max's usefulness wound is present;
- his growth is demonstrated through restraint.

Mystery:
- geometry appears worth measuring but proves nothing yet.

System:
- no expanded mobility;
- no portable communication.

Ending:
Max marks the outer permitted point in his notebook and labels everything beyond it **NOT DATA**.

## CH-023 — Behind the East Door

**POV:** Lily  
**Movement:** A — Permission to Enter

Purpose:
- enter LOC-006 legitimately;
- establish physical corridor texture and ordinary maintenance stakes;
- collect a first low-risk baseline.

Core beats:
- Inez unlocks the East Maintenance Corridor;
- Lily accompanies the inspection as the reporting tenant and measurement helper;
- the corridor contains old conduits, service recesses, temperature variation, and old material changes;
- Lily notices fixed geometry that might be comparable to Max's Larkspur range;
- she does not secretly perform an unauthorized dimensional test.

Supporting cast:

Ending:
Lily spots an old recess in roughly the position Max predicted—but refuses to call it a match without measurements.

## CH-024 — Your Measurements Are Wrong

**POV:** Max  
**Movement:** B — Measure Twice

Purpose:
- begin independent comparison;
- use comedy from terminology and measurement conventions;
- keep evidence inconclusive.

Core beats:
- Lily and Max compare their first measurement sets at the base;
- units, construction terms, and bad sketches create friction and comedy;
- the data suggests similar relative geometry but contains enough error to prevent a conclusion;
- Max proposes standardized reference points.

Relationship:
- they operate as field partners but still argue like roommates.

Mystery:
- M-007 remains unactivated in canon unless manuscript evidence later justifies activation.

Ending:
They discover they measured from different reference surfaces and have to redo almost everything.

## CH-027 — Same Mark

**POV:** Lily  
**Movement:** C — Midpoint

Purpose:
- deliver the arc midpoint;
- authorize F-005 planting only if prose depicts the matching physical evidence;
- move M-007 from possibility into an active local mystery through actual evidence.

Core beats:
- during a legitimate return to LOC-006, Lily finds two short vertical notches crossed by one shallow diagonal score on old structural material;
- she records orientation, height, and surrounding geometry before reading Max's full description;
- back at Unit 4B, she compares her record with Max's;
- the marks match in relative position and form closely enough that random coincidence becomes difficult to defend.

Mystery state if manuscript supports it:
- **F-005 — Shared Foundation Mark: PLANTED**
- **M-007: ACTIVE**

Interpretation ceiling:
They may conclude only that the deeper match is unlikely to be random.

They may not conclude:
- one original building;
- one original world;
- Twinfall;
- Threshold Keeper involvement.

Ending:
For the first time, Lily writes: **THE ROOMS AREN'T THE ONLY THING MATCHING.**
