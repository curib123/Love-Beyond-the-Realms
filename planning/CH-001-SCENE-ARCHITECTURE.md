# 1. Chapter Function

CH-001 establishes Lily before the romance/mystery engine fully begins.

The chapter must:
- make Unit 4B feel like a choice Lily wants to succeed;
- establish Lily's practical, dry, emotionally careful voice;
- show her independence as both strength and flaw;
- introduce Aster House as old but believable;
- show that Nora cares without making Nora controlling or cruel;
- include one ambiguous apartment inconsistency that can still be dismissed;
- end with Lily seeing Max inside Unit 4B;
- stop before Lily and Max communicate.

The chapter must **not** behave like a lore prologue.

# 2. Chapter Knowledge Ceiling

At the start of CH-001 Lily knows:
- she has rented Unit 4B;
- Aster House is old and imperfect;
- she wants to live independently;
- Nora worries about her;
- she works at Paper Moon;
- her parents died eight months earlier in an ordinary road accident.

Lily does **not** know:
- Max;
- Velaire;
- Echo Exchange;
- Phase Lag;
- mirrorstone;
- the Veil;
- the Twinfall;
- dimensional overlap;
- why Unit 4B is unusual.

Writer-side truth must not enter Lily's narration.

# 3. Mystery / Foreshadowing Ceiling

Authorized mystery information:
- one small, ambiguous room inconsistency;
- Max's visual appearance in the final scene.

Mystery state:
- M-001 remains **UNASKED** through most of the chapter;
- the final sight of Max creates the event that will make M-001 ACTIVE in CH-002.

Approved inconsistency:

- later she notices it standing slightly open again;

# 6. Scene Architecture

## SC-001-01 — The Key Works

**POV:** Lily  
**Location:** Aster House entrance / elevator / Unit 4B entry  
**Present Characters:** Lily, brief Inez Bell presence  
**Approximate Function:** Arrival and ownership

### Purpose

Establish:
- Aster House as old, ordinary, and imperfect;
- Lily's practical voice;
- the apartment as a deliberate step toward independence;
- Inez as a pragmatic landlord rather than a spooky exposition source.

### Immediate Want

Lily wants to get herself and the last of her boxes into Unit 4B without accepting more help than necessary.

### Obstacle

Aster House is inconvenient:
- old elevator;
- awkward box;
- sticky lock or stubborn door;
- Inez offers one practical warning/helpful instruction.

The obstacle must feel normal.

### Dialogue Purpose

Inez's brief dialogue should:
- establish ordinary building pragmatism;
- give Lily a natural person to respond to;
- avoid any "this apartment is haunted" hint;
- show Lily politely refusing unnecessary help.

Inez may say something ordinary about:
- the elevator;
- trash collection;
- a window latch;
- calling if the plumbing acts up.

No previous-tenant story in CH-001.

### Comedy Opportunity

Very light situational humor:
- Lily picked an apartment with an elevator that sounds less confident than she feels;
- a box labeled "KITCHEN" contains something obviously not useful to the kitchen because of rushed packing.

The humor should come through Lily's dry observation, not a joke routine.

### Character Movement

Lily experiences a small private satisfaction when the key finally opens her own door.

She does not think "this will fix me."

The emotional meaning is:
- this is hers;
- she chose it;
- she wants it to work.

### Mystery Information

None.

### Sci-Fi Rule

No visible sci-fi event.

### Physical Change

Lily enters Unit 4B with her belongings.

### Exit Condition

Inez leaves.

The apartment door closes.

For the first time, Lily is alone inside a home that belongs to her.

This directly causes SC-001-02: Lily begins making the empty apartment livable.

## SC-001-02 — Make It Livable

**POV:** Lily  
**Location:** Unit 4B — living/dining room, kitchen, bedroom  
**Present Characters:** Lily only  
**Approximate Function:** Domestic grounding + independence flaw

### Purpose

Make Unit 4B emotionally real before it becomes impossible.

Establish through action:
- Lily likes order;
- she notices domestic details;
- she solves problems practically;
- she refuses help too quickly.

### Immediate Want

Lily wants to get the essential parts of the apartment functional:
- bed;
- kettle or basic kitchen setup;
- work clothes;
- phone charger;
- one place to sit and eat.

She does not need to fully unpack.

### Obstacle

One task is annoying enough that Nora's help would genuinely make it easier.

Approved example:
- a flat-packed shelf or small table is awkward to hold level alone;
- Lily improvises a solution with boxes/books rather than calling Nora.

The task must be safe. Do not make Lily perform obviously dangerous lifting just to prove independence.

### Dialogue Purpose

No full conversation required.

Optional short muttered lines from Lily can show voice.

A phone notification from Nora may appear, but Lily does not answer yet.

### Comedy Opportunity

Lily's practical workaround succeeds in a technically functional but ugly way.

Her reaction should be dry rather than slapstick.

### Character Movement

Show the flaw behind the strength:
- Lily is capable;
- Lily also treats needing simple help as something to avoid.

Do not explain the false belief directly.

### Mystery Information

None yet.

### Sci-Fi Rule

No explicit dimensional effect.

### Physical Change

The apartment becomes partially lived-in:
- essentials unpacked;
- one corner functional;
- built-in cabinet used and deliberately closed.

### Exit Condition

Nora calls or sends a message that is difficult to keep ignoring.

This directly causes SC-001-03.

## SC-001-03 — I'm Fine

**POV:** Lily  
**Location:** Unit 4B — kitchen/living room  
**Present Characters:** Lily; Nora by phone  
**Approximate Function:** Family pressure + first ambiguous inconsistency

### Purpose

Establish:
- Nora genuinely cares;
- Lily dislikes being treated as fragile;
- their conflict is about safety versus independence;
- Lily's grief exists in the background without dominating the scene.

Introduce one small apartment inconsistency.

### Immediate Want

Lily wants to reassure Nora quickly so she can continue settling in without being asked to come back home.

### Obstacle

Nora asks practical questions Lily interprets as doubt:
- Did the landlord fix the window?
- Is the lock good?
- Did Lily eat?
- Does she want Nora to come by?

Nora should not guilt Lily over her parents.

### Dialogue Purpose

The conversation should reveal relationship dynamics through normal speech.

Nora:
- warm;
- practical;
- careful not to command.

Lily:
- concise;
- mildly defensive;
- uses "I'm fine" or equivalent too quickly;
- changes subject when concern gets emotionally close.

No grief speech.

### Comedy Opportunity

Small domestic humor is allowed:
- Nora correctly guesses Lily has not eaten a proper meal;
- Lily tries to defend something barely qualifying as dinner.

The humor should soften the relationship, not mock Nora's concern.

### Character Movement

Lily does **not** agree to move back.

But she makes one small concession, such as:
- promising to text after her next work shift;
- agreeing Nora can visit later;
- accepting one practical suggestion.

This prevents independence from becoming simple rejection of connection.

### Mystery Information

After or during the call, Lily notices the built-in cabinet she deliberately closed in SC-001-02 is slightly open again.

Her reasonable interpretations:
- bad hinge;
- uneven floor;
- she did not close it as firmly as she thought.

She closes it again.

Important:
- no cold key;
- no wrong furniture;
- no mirror anomaly;
- no sound from Max;
- no writer confirmation of cause.

### Sci-Fi Rule

The event may be compatible with the room's instability, but the prose remains observational and ambiguous.

### Physical Change

Cabinet is closed again.

Lily continues preparing the apartment for the evening.

### Exit Condition

The call ends.

Lily decides she is done solving problems for the night and begins one final ordinary routine.

This directly causes SC-001-04.

## SC-001-04 — Not Alone

**POV:** Lily  
**Location:** Unit 4B — bedroom/living room sightline  
**Present Characters:** Lily; Max visible only  
**Approximate Function:** Chapter hook / first impossible event

### Purpose

Break the chapter's established normality with one clear impossible image.

The scene should not answer what Max is.

### Immediate Want

Lily wants to finish one last ordinary task and stop for the night.

Approved ordinary actions:
- set out clothes for work;
- plug in her phone;
- get water;
- check the front lock;
- turn off lights.

### Obstacle

At first, none.

The ordinary routine is the setup for the visual break.

### Dialogue Purpose

No Lily/Max conversation.

Lily may say one brief line to herself if natural, but silence is preferable once she notices him.

Max does not speak to Lily in this chapter.

### Comedy Opportunity

None required.

The ending should be strange, not undercut by a joke.

### Character Movement

Lily's sense of ownership and privacy is immediately challenged.

She has spent the chapter making the apartment "hers."

Now someone impossible appears inside it.

### Mystery Information

Lily sees a young man inside the apartment.

Presentation rules:
- he appears physically real enough to trigger "intruder" before "ghost";
- Lily has no explanation;
- do not describe Velaire technology in a way Lily can correctly identify;
- do not establish his name;
- do not establish whether he can see or hear her;
- do not establish that he is alive;
- do not establish that he is from another world.

This is the event that hands directly into CH-002.

### Sci-Fi Rule

P0 permits Lily to see Max.

No other P0 capability is demonstrated yet.

### Physical Change

None required.

The power of the beat is perceptual, not destructive.

### Exit Condition

Lily realizes there is a young man standing or moving inside a locked apartment where no one should be.

**End chapter immediately after the recognition lands.**

CH-002 begins with Lily deciding what he might be and how to respond.
