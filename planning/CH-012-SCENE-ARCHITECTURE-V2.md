# 3. Core Ordinary Consequence

The old wall mirror's mounting bracket has loosened in aging plaster because Lily has repeatedly re-angled the heavy fixture for P1 calibration.

This is an **ordinary mechanical consequence**.

It is NOT:
- Echo Exchange;
- mirrorstone behavior;
- a new clue;
- MT-003 evidence;
- a dimensional attack.

Lily notices the mirror shift slightly and immediately stops using it.

She reports the repair to Inez rather than hiding damage to preserve access to Max.

That choice is the chapter's key independence/responsibility beat.

# 4. Rent / Building Pressure

# 7. Detailed Scene Architecture

## SC-012-01 — Rent Is Still Real

**POV:** Lily  
**Location:** Unit 4B — morning  
**Present:** Lily  
**Purpose:** establish sleep/rent/repair problem before dimensional plot can dominate

### Immediate Want
Get through an ordinary morning and keep Unit 4B functioning.

### Beats
- Lily wakes later than intended after staying up too long reviewing visual-calibration notes.
- She pays/checks her rent on time.
- While straightening the room, she notices the old wall mirror shift slightly when touched.
- Inspection shows one mounting point has loosened in old plaster.
- She connects this to the repeated physical re-angling done during their P1 tests, not to a supernatural event.
- She immediately decides not to use the mirror again until it is repaired.

### Character Beat
Lily is tempted to think she can manage it herself, but chooses to report it to Inez because hiding building damage would contradict the independence she is trying to prove she can handle.

### Comedy
Rent remains more reliably frightening than dimensional physics.

Keep it dry and brief.

### Mystery / System
No new mystery evidence.
No visual test occurs.

### Exit
Lily contacts Inez and requests a repair.

## SC-012-02 — Mrs. Bell Has Questions

**POV:** Lily  
**Location:** Unit 4B  
**Present:** Lily, Inez Bell  
**Purpose:** landlord/building pressure; ordinary consequences gain teeth

### Inez's Independent Goal
Keep Aster House safe, repairable, inspectable, and financially viable.

### Beats
- Inez checks the mirror mount and old plaster.
- She sees floor tape / moved furniture / experiment-related room arrangement but has no reason to infer dimensional activity.
- Lily gives truthful ordinary facts: she has been repositioning the mirror and furniture.
- Inez is mildly exasperated, not suspicious or sinister.
- Inez schedules repair/maintenance and mentions an ordinary upcoming building/insurance inspection.
- Unit 4B needs to remain accessible and safe.
- Inez may warn Lily not to keep shifting heavy old fixtures.
- Lily's rent/payment responsibility is acknowledged; this is not an eviction threat.

### Important Limit
Inez must NOT:
- reveal prior tenant haunting reports;
- mention the East Maintenance Corridor as a mystery lead;
- reveal building-history clues;
- recognize Echo Exchange;
- imply Lily is causing dimensional damage.

### Character Beat
Lily realizes keeping 4B means accepting landlord rules, repairs, and outside access to the space.

Independence is responsibility, not isolation.

### Exit
The wall mirror is temporarily unavailable as a P1 receiver until repaired.

This is **mundane access loss**, not system regression.

## SC-012-03 — You Can Stay With Me

**POV:** Lily  
**Location:** Unit 4B / phone call or planned brief visit with Nora  
**Present:** Lily, Nora  
**Purpose:** family safety-vs-independence pressure

### Trigger
Nora learns:
- Lily is tired;
- a repair/inspection is happening;
- Lily has been sleeping poorly.

Nora does not know dimensional truth.

### Nora's Position
She offers practical support:
- dinner;
- a place to sleep for one night if repair access becomes disruptive;
- help with errands if Lily needs it.

She does NOT order Lily home.

### Lily's Initial Reaction
Lily reflexively says she is fine and interprets the offer as possible monitoring.

### Corrective Beat
Nora makes clear:
- Lily can stay in 4B;
- the offer is not a judgment;
- independence does not require refusing every form of help.

### Lily's Choice
Lily does not move back.

She accepts one small form of support, such as:
- dinner with Nora;
- groceries;
- using Nora's place only if the repair requires it.

This is modest character movement, not a full resolution of Lily's false belief.

### Mystery / Romance
No dimensional exposition to Nora.
No jealousy.
No Max explanation.

### Exit
Lily chooses to keep Unit 4B while also accepting that she has an Earth-side support system.

## SC-012-04 — The Apartment Comes First

**POV:** Lily  
**Location:** Unit 4B — Correlation Patch A  
**Present:** Lily; Max through bounded writing/audio only  
**Purpose:** translate ordinary pressure into Lily/Max relationship rules

### Communication State
Writing is preferred because the wall mirror is unavailable for repair.

A brief ordinary audio window is allowed if natural, but no new system behavior may result.

### Lily Tells Max
- the mirror needs repair;
- no visual calibration until it is fixed;
- building inspection/repair access takes priority;
- no more late-night tests on work nights;
- furniture and fixtures must be restored after experiments.

### Max Response
He accepts the boundary.

He may joke about landlords / rent / the apparent power of Mrs. Bell, but he does not:
- guilt Lily;
- suggest hiding damage;
- pressure her to preserve P1;
- treat Earth responsibilities as less important than his problem.

### Relationship Beat
Max's willingness to lose convenient visual access rather than endanger Lily's home deepens trust.

Lily recognizes that he understands Unit 4B is her home, not merely their experiment site.

### Closing Beat
Possible locked direction:

Lily looks at the temporarily unusable mirror and realizes that not seeing Max for a few days is disappointing—but keeping the apartment safe is the reason there is a place for them to meet at all.

End on responsibility plus quiet attachment, not a cliffhanger.
