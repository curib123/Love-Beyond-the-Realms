# 1. Chapter Function

CH-022 defines Max's safe Larkspur investigation range before Lily enters the East Maintenance Corridor.

The chapter is not primarily about mapping Larkspur.

Its emotional purpose is:

**Max proves that he can stop before usefulness turns into self-sacrifice.**

The sci-fi survey exists to pressure that choice.

The relationship purpose is:

**Max takes Lily's safety boundary seriously even when she is not physically present to enforce it.**

The ordinary-life purpose is:

**Max prepares, eats/rests, works within his phase-bound reality, and treats recovery as part of the task rather than wasted time.**

The chapter's meaningful change is:

**Before CH-022:** Max knows he is phase-bound but has not defined a practical Arc 2 survey boundary.

**After CH-022:** Max has a documented safe field range, a symptom-based stop condition, and a fixed outer point beyond which he will not treat observations as usable data.

# 6. Scene Architecture

## Scene 1 — Breakfast Before Cartography

**POV:** Max  
**Location:** Room 17  
**Approximate manuscript weight:** 450–650 words

### Scene Purpose

Re-establish Max as a person before he becomes an investigator.

Show:
- ordinary routine;
- physical preparation;
- Lily/Max chemistry;
- the promise from CH-021 becoming behavior.

### Entry State

Max wakes after enough rest to make the survey responsible.

He does not begin immediately.

He:
- drinks water;
- eats something simple;
- checks whether he has residual headache/nausea;
- organizes notebook, measuring cord/tape, pencil/chalk, and a simple distance reference;
- makes sure he has a clear return path.

The exact Velaire food/device names should remain simple unless already canonized.

### Slice-of-Life Texture

Room 17 should not feel like a laboratory.

Include ordinary details:
- untidy bedding;
- a cup or plate;
- clothes drying / folded badly;
- distant building noises;
- signs of a life interrupted but still being lived.

Max may hear ordinary Larkspur activity he cannot fully participate in.

This should quietly reinforce his phase-bound condition without turning into exposition.

### Lily/Max P0 Beat

Before leaving the base, Max checks the writing patch.

Lily is preparing for her own morning / inspection.

Their exchange should be brief and character-driven.

Useful content:
- Lily asks whether he slept;
- Max tries to classify "mostly" as a valid answer;
- Lily refuses;
- Max admits the real amount / confirms he is physically okay;
- Lily repeats only the essential boundary: **stop at symptoms, not at ambition** or equivalent simple phrasing.

Max may tease her for turning his survey into a health form.

Lily may remind him that he volunteered for the rule.

### Romantic-Comedy / Chemistry Function

This is not flirting-heavy.

The chemistry comes from:
- Lily knowing his habits;
- Max anticipating which answer she will reject;
- domestic familiarity across realities;
- both treating safety as shared routine.

### Character Turn

Max privately recognizes that Lily will not know what happens in the passage until he returns.

That means the rule only matters if **he** chooses to follow it.

He writes one clear stop condition in his notebook before leaving:

**FIRST REAL SYMPTOM = RETURN.**

No "five more seconds."

### Scene End

Max leaves the reliable Room 17 base and enters the adjoining service passage.

No cross-world communication follows him.

## Scene 2 — The Point Where Useful Stops

**POV:** Max  
**Location:** Room 17 doorway → adjoining Larkspur service passage → first structural junction / inspection alcove  
**Approximate manuscript weight:** 650–900 words

### Scene Purpose

Define the actual field boundary while turning Max's usefulness flaw into a choice.

### Survey Method

Keep method simple.

Max records only:
- distance from Room 17 threshold;
- major fixed turns;
- wall/junction position;
- first onset of symptoms;
- return distance.

He is not yet comparing Larkspur to Aster.

He is establishing:
**where he can safely stand and still trust his observations.**

### Field Range

Allowed sequence:

1. Room 17 threshold — stable.
2. Direct adjoining service passage — conditionally stable.
3. First structural junction / inspection alcove — outer permitted point.

No farther travel.

### Sensory Progression

The passage should begin ordinary enough:
- old wall;
- utility/service fixtures;
- stale air or dust;
- distant building noise.

As Max approaches the first junction, symptoms begin in a controlled sequence.

Recommended first symptom:
**pressure behind the eyes / slight visual doubling at hard edges.**

Optional secondary warning after he stops moving:
**brief nausea or balance lag.**

Do not stack dramatic symptoms.

No supernatural spectacle is required.

### Mystery Ceiling

Max may notice that the passage contains fixed structural features worth measuring later.

He may NOT:
- identify a matching Aster feature;
- find F-005;
- conclude deeper structural correspondence;
- interpret the symptom boundary as evidence of a major twist.

### Core Character Test

At the first symptom, Max stops.

Important internal pressure:
- the junction is close;
- one more step could give a cleaner measurement;
- he wants something useful to hand Lily before her inspection;
- his old instinct says one more step is manageable.

Then the relational/character counterweight:
- Lily's `NO IMPROVISING`;
- his own `NO FIVE MORE SECONDS`;
- the fact that lying about the limit would make the later comparison unsafe for both of them.

He chooses to turn back.

This is the chapter's real climax.

### Romance-First Rule

The decision must not be framed as:
**Max obeys Lily because Lily told him to.**

It must be:
**Max respects a boundary they built together and chooses to be someone she can trust.**

Lily's absence makes the choice stronger.

### Technical Result

Max records:
- Room 17 = reliable base;
- adjoining service passage = usable under current condition;
- first structural junction / inspection alcove = symptom boundary / outer permitted point;
- anything beyond = unverified and unusable.

If the first symptom starts slightly before the physical junction, the symptom point becomes the effective boundary.

For chapter continuity, preferred execution:
**symptoms begin at the first structural junction / inspection alcove itself**, allowing the architecture's maximum point to remain the defined outer limit without expanding beyond it.

### Scene End

Max returns to Room 17 before trying to turn the symptom into more data.

The return should feel mildly frustrating but physically successful.

## Scene 3 — Not Data

**POV:** Max  
**Location:** Room 17  
**Approximate manuscript weight:** 500–700 words

### Scene Purpose

Show recovery, honesty, Lily/Max trust, and convert the field experience into a usable protocol.

### Recovery First

Before writing to Lily, Max:
- sits;
- drinks water;
- waits for visual pressure/doubling to clear;
- records how long recovery takes.

He does not immediately chase the next task.

This makes recovery part of the research discipline.

### P0 Report

Once stable, Max uses the Room 17 writing patch.

He reports the real result:
- he reached the first junction;
- symptoms began there;
- he stopped;
- he came back;
- he did not test beyond it.

Lily's first response should prioritize whether he is okay before asking for measurements.

That supports the romance/relationship hierarchy.

Max notices this.

Do not turn the beat into a confession.

### Comedy / Chemistry

After Lily confirms he is physically okay, she may ask for the practical result.

Max may present an unnecessarily formal boundary label.

Lily may shorten it.

Example dynamic:
- Max: **CONDITIONALLY RELIABLE OUTER SURVEY LIMIT**
- Lily: **SAFE DISTANCE**
- Max: objects that hers sounds less impressive.
- Lily: points out it is also the chapter's useful phrase.

Do not meta-reference the chapter title in prose.

### Protocol Creation

Max creates a simple future field rule:
1. start from Room 17;
2. measure only fixed features inside known safe range;
3. first symptom means return;
4. no observations beyond the stop point count as usable data;
5. recover before comparison.

This protocol should be readable in story prose, not dumped as a long numbered manual unless formatting naturally fits.

### Relationship Movement

The meaningful relationship change is small but real:

Before:
Lily had to tell Max not to improvise.

After:
Max demonstrates he can enforce the rule himself and report the limitation honestly.

This strengthens:
**roommate trust → field-partner trust**

without changing the formal R2 state.

### Final Image / Ending

Max marks the outer point in his notebook.

He draws a line after the first junction / inspection alcove.

Beyond it he writes:

**NOT DATA**

The phrase matters because it rejects his old instinct to treat every dangerous observation as useful.

Ending should directly prepare CH-023:
Lily now has a safe Max-side reference envelope before entering the East Maintenance Corridor.
