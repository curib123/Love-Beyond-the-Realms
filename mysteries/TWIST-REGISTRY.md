# Plot Twist Registry

**Owner:** A053 Plot Twist Editor  
**Status:** CANON — TWIST CONTROL

This file manages surprises without turning the story into random shock reveals.

# 1. Twist Classes

## MINOR

A local reversal or reinterpretation.

A minor twist may:
- change the meaning of one clue;
- reveal a misunderstanding;
- expose a small secret;
- change an immediate plan;
- turn comedy into useful evidence.

## MAJOR

A series-level reinterpretation.

A major twist must:
- be foreshadowed;
- affect both Lily and Max;
- change the central understanding;
- produce a new choice or conflict;
- remain consistent on reread.

# 2. Locked Major Twists

## MT-001 — The Worlds Were Once One

**Status:** LOCKED WRITER TRUTH — UNREVEALED

Earth and Velaire are two histories created by the Twinfall.

They are not naturally unrelated parallel universes.

Required setup paths:
- shared old geometry;
- map similarities;
- matching construction marks;
- Echo Exchange behavior inconsistent with ordinary portals.

Reveal window:
- only after readers understand basic overlap rules.

Effect:
- changes the story question from "How do we cross to another universe?" to "Why was one world split, and should the branches reconnect?"

## MT-002 — The Present Overlap Was Reactivated

**Status:** LOCKED CONCEPT — RESPONSIBLE PERSON NOT YET LOCKED

The current connection was strengthened by a deliberate Velaire-side experiment.

The experiment did not intend to trap Max.

Required setup:
- active instrument residue;
- missing archive record;
- evidence that the overlap began too cleanly to be random.

Effect:
- creates a human mystery and responsibility question.

Do not lock the responsible character until Character/Mystery QA approves the motive.

## MT-003 — Their Experiments Strengthen the Leak

**Status:** LOCKED WRITER TRUTH — UNREVEALED

Lily and Max believe repeated tests are only helping them understand the overlap.

In reality, repeated coherence tests also strengthen the local correlation.

Required setup:
- Shared Minutes slowly lengthen;
- object exchanges become easier after tests;
- recovery periods shorten while instability spreads outward.

Effect:
- their progress creates the problem they later must help solve.

This is consequence, not punishment for falling in love.

# 3. Protected Future Major-Twist Candidates

These are **PROVISIONAL**, not canon.

## MTC-004 — Old Earth Contact

A previous Aster House resident may have documented Velaire decades earlier.

Potential use:
- prove the present pair are not the first contact;
- reveal a failed earlier attempt;
- connect Earth-side mystery to old tenant history.

## MTC-005 — More Than One Node

Other branch-correlated locations may exist.

Potential use:
- expand beyond one apartment;
- allow future arcs without arbitrary new portals.

## MTC-006 — Twinfall Motive

The original experiment may have had a purpose different from what surviving Velaire history claims.

Do not define the final answer until later world-history development requires it.

# 4. Minor Twist Pool

These are reusable patterns, not fixed chapter assignments.

- Max seems to ignore Lily, but Phase Lag delayed the message.
- A "haunted" object belongs to neither current room; it is a state echo from an older configuration.
- Lily catches Max hiding something, but it is an embarrassing personal object rather than a sinister secret.
- A scary voice in the room is Mina or Elara searching on the Velaire side.
- Ava thinks Lily has a secret boyfriend in the apartment and draws the wrong conclusion for several chapters.
- Max thinks an Earth device is a scientific instrument when it is something ordinary.
- A clue assumed to come from Velaire is actually an old Earth-side mark.
- Corven appears to sabotage contact but is preventing a dangerous coherence spike.
- A romantic interruption that looks random later proves to be a measurable phase event.

# 5. Twist Spacing Rule

Do not turn every chapter into a twist.

A story also needs:
- setup;
- normal life;
- romance;
- comedy;
- consequence;
- quiet chapters.

Minor twists should vary in size.

Major twists need breathing room afterward.

# 6. Reveal Fairness

Before a major reveal:
- at least two independent clue paths;
- at least one clue readers could notice without specialist knowledge;
- no false narration from a truthful POV solely to hide the answer.

# 7. Emotional Requirement

A major twist is incomplete until it changes a relationship or choice.

Knowledge alone is not enough.
